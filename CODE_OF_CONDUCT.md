# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project.

Be considerate, discuss technical trade-offs in good faith, and give actionable
feedback. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publication of
private workbook data are not accepted.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation
when necessary to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, keep details limited to those who need them, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
